# Moon Diner — series bible

**Tone:** quiet, deadpan comedy. Small stakes, big feelings.
**Look:** flat paper-cutout characters (cutout_2d) in a perspective night diner.

## Places
- **The diner** (`diner`): 8 m x 6 m. Counter along the north wall, three stools, a pie case, a clock that is always wrong.
  The door is on the west wall.

## Characters
- **Marta** (9): stubborn, impatient. Red beanie, never takes it off. Sits on the middle stool (`stool_2`).
- **Blob** (40): the cook. Nervous, kind, always forgets orders. Chef hat and apron.

## Continuity rules
- Marta always wears the beanie.
- The pie is never actually served.
- Blob looks at the clock when he is stalling.

## How to write scenes (for the AI)
1. Read `vocabulary.md` first and use only words from it.
2. Declare everything with `use`, place characters at the top of the scene, then direct the action.
3. Run `oneiro check` after each change and fix every diagnostic before rendering.
